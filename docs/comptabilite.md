# Comptabilité

**Tous mes outils → Comptabilité** opens the accounting workspace. In account mode, this workspace, its records and original documents are owner-only; clients and trusted carers cannot read or change them. Its journal, customer invoices, purchases/expense claims, review queue, and bookings/rates stay in the same workspace. Existing planned monthly booking totals and configured rates remain under **Réservations et tarifs**; home/planning shortcuts open that tab directly.

Open **Rechercher une écriture** to search party names, references, notes and line descriptions, or filter by month. Search ignores case; the month filters the record date, not service dates, due dates or payment dates. Totals follow the selected tab and filters, including all recorded payments on those entries regardless of payment date. The booking-summary month is separate. Open a row to review its lines, payment history, recognized text and original document.

## Everyday flow

- Create a customer invoice with **Nouvelle facture**, or choose **Ajouter un frais** and select the type for a supplier bill, expense claim or billed extra. Enter the party, date, currency, lines and any explicitly known tax included in the gross prices. Unknown amounts stay incomplete. **Réutiliser ces coordonnées pour les prochaines factures** saves issuer details for future invoices when the record is saved.
- **Ajouter une réservation enregistrée** copies a saved booking's full service units, agreed unit rate and currency into an editable line, and fills an empty party name from its saved family association in account mode, falling back to the dog's current client only when no association exists. An unavailable historical family name stays empty. Cancelled bookings and bookings already referenced by a non-cancelled record or the open editor are excluded from the picker. A cancelled booking cannot supply new charge lines or be issued from a previously saved draft. Its saved invoice lines and payments remain factual and unchanged by cancellation. Lines in one record must share a currency; unknown booking prices remain incomplete. Later booking edits/extensions do not update copied invoice lines. In account mode, current [quoted booking options](client-portal.md#prices-and-options) are copied into separate editable lines. Later option changes do not alter the invoice; additional invoice lines remain available.
- Save a draft, then review before issuing/confirming. Customer invoice number and issuer/customer details are required at issuance. Issued details cannot be rewritten, and their numbers cannot be reused. Unpaid records may be cancelled with a reason; originals and cancelled records remain available. Record actual partial/full payments or reimbursements manually; first save or discard any pending record edits. The app does not execute banking operations or send invoices.
- Converting a reviewed purchase, expense or extra into a customer invoice remains editable and unissued until validation and saving succeed. An unsuccessful issuance attempt still allows an incomplete unpaid draft to be saved. Records with existing payments must remain confirmed when saved; converting a paid extra requires successful issuance and retains its payments and currency.
- Open a saved issued or cancelled customer invoice and choose **Imprimer / enregistrer en PDF**. This opens the browser print dialog; choose a printer or its PDF option. Draft invoices have no print control. The document includes the saved status, details, lines, gross total, optional included tax, recorded paid amount, note and cancellation reason where present. It is not automatically sent or stored as another original.
- Open **Ajouter des justificatifs**, then **Prendre une photo** or **Choisir des photos ou PDF** to add JPEG/PNG photos or PDFs. The photo control requests rear-facing capture where supported; file selection remains available. Recognition runs on the device using bundled assets, without an external document service. A photo of multiple pale receipts on a darker table can suggest separate areas; adjust/remove areas using drawing or percentage fields. Each selected area/page becomes an editable review draft. Check multi-page invoices carefully: pages are separate draft entries, not automatically reconciled into one invoice.
- Check proposed party, date, reference, currency, category and amount against the retained original. Ambiguous values remain incomplete. When the first nonblank line is a recognized whole card-network heading (such as Mastercard or Visa), the supplier stays blank; names containing those words, such as **Visa Services**, remain eligible supplier proposals. Complete missing details from evidence during review. The reader proposes a single distinct supported reference following **Facture**, **Invoice**, **Rechnung**, **Fattura** or **Factura**, including supported wrapped layouts. For example, **Facture F-701** proposes **F-701**, while an invoice heading followed by **Date 06.10.2026** or **TOTAL CHF 42.50** leaves the reference blank; conflicting references also stay blank. These rules apply only to new OCR proposals and do not rewrite saved or manually reviewed values. Supported date, amount and currency proposals, literal recognized text and the original/page/region link remain available; missing address and tax details stay empty. OCR is fallible; suggestions never become confirmed records automatically.
- If recognition is unavailable, retain originals with manual drafts. PDF previews load near the visible page; OCR renders and releases one page region at a time. If PDF loading fails, a manual draft retains the eligible original without inventing a page or region. A later preview failure keeps known pages and edited regions available for manual drafts; known page-count limits still reject oversized PDFs. Duplicate original bytes are rejected to avoid accidental re-import.
- **Exporter pour le comptable** downloads `comptabilite.zip` containing `comptabilite.xlsx` (journal, line items, payments, source manifest, guidance), `records.json` (the exact saved finance snapshot, including recognized text/regions), and every original under `originals/<sha256>.<extension>`. Extract the whole ZIP together so the workbook's relative source links work. Sheet names, statuses and most column headings use the current UI language; technical labels such as `Booking ID`, `Payment ID` and `Bytes` remain fixed. IDs, dates and user text remain literal strings, never formulas. Amounts and quantities are numeric cells; unknown amounts are blank. The manifest retains original filenames. Missing or mismatched originals fail the whole export rather than silently omitting evidence. Export covers all saved entries, including drafts/cancellations, regardless of the current filter; save pending edits/intake first. This archive has no restore/import control in the app.

Planned bookings, confirmed invoices/expenses and actual recorded cash movements are different measures. Totals exclude drafts/cancellations and are separated by currency. This is preparation for accounting review, not automatic tax assessment, bank reconciliation or tax filing.

## Storage and contracts

`finance-model.js` and `api/finance.py` define matching snapshot contracts. Amounts use integer hundredths, displayed and printed with two decimal places for every accepted currency. Gross line amounts are quantity × unit price; unknown unit prices remain unknown in print, including cancelled drafts. The optional included-tax field is informational and is not added again. Payments are append-only, cannot exceed the record total and cannot be silently removed. Once a payment exists, its record must retain its currency and income/expense direction. Existing issued invoice content and source metadata are immutable.

Account mode uses additive `business_finance` and `finance_document` tables in the private SQLite database. Existing businesses start with an empty accounting snapshot. Reads share the account snapshot transaction; writes commit the snapshot and original BLOBs atomically under the existing business/revision guards. See the [API contract](#api-contract) below. No document or recognized text is sent to an external reader; account saves do send those records and original bytes to the configured account server.

Static mode uses the separate IndexedDB database `dogcare-finance-v1`. Snapshot and original files commit in one readwrite transaction. Comparing the loaded baseline inside that transaction rejects stale-tab overwrites. Existing localStorage keys are unchanged, and there is no automatic finance import into an account. Use a current browser on HTTPS or localhost for the secure browser APIs used by record IDs and file hashing. Storage failure leaves the form/import draft available; save before closing/reloading. Browser storage is not a server backup: export copies deliberately. Static-mode demo reset leaves accounting records and originals intact; account Settings has no demo reset.

Accounting tabs, booking/rate shortcuts and other routes retain the open editor, payment/cancellation inputs and selected originals in memory. Resume controls return to that work; opening another record requires saving or deliberately discarding pending edits. Editor totals follow the current inputs after navigation or language changes; incomplete or invalid amounts remain unknown. These drafts are not stored across sessions. Export compresses originals one at a time in a local worker, retaining completed output as Blob parts; forms stay editable, and the download starts only after the complete archive succeeds. Exports that reach ZIP32 size or offset capacity, including archive metadata, fail visibly without a partial download; saved records, originals and open drafts remain available. Its snapshot and workbook language are fixed when export starts. If accounting cannot load, its journal, mutations and export remain unavailable with a retry control; other routes and bookings/rates remain accessible. Account retries retain the loaded business and revision guards.

Unavailable or malformed daily records disable booking integration without blocking manual invoices or expenses. Currency stays incomplete until entered manually; existing daily records remain unchanged.

Limits: 5 MiB per original, 10 files/20 MiB per intake, 20 pages per PDF, 40 million pixels per image (encoded PNG/JPEG dimensions checked before decoding, then checked again after decoding), 5,000 entries and 5,000 originals, 100 lines/payments per entry. Oversized files, unsupported formats (including HEIC) and images that cannot be decoded reject the intake batch without saving it. Eligible PDFs that cannot be rendered can use the manual-original fallback described above. Camera intake accepts JPEG/PNG; it does not convert unsupported formats. Paper-area detection is a suggestion, not guaranteed document separation. OCR uses the current interface language's bundled reader (French, English, Italian, German or Spanish).

## API contract

With an owner session, read `finance` and `revision` from `GET /api/state`, then send `PUT /api/finance` with exactly `finance` and `uploads`, the session cookie, `X-DogCare-Business: <business_id>` and `If-Match: "<revision>"` as described in the [mutation contract](../README.md#mutation-contract). Other roles receive `finance: null` in state and cannot write finance snapshots or download originals. `finance` is a **full snapshot**, not a patch: every previously saved entry and original must remain present. There is no deletion endpoint. Use `uploads: []` when adding no originals. The initial empty shape is:

```json
{
  "finance": {
    "version": 1,
    "profile": { "name": "", "address": "", "taxId": "" },
    "entries": [],
    "documents": []
  },
  "uploads": []
}
```

All fields are required and extra fields are rejected. `profile` holds reusable issuer details: `name` up to 160 Unicode code points, `address` up to 1,000 and `taxId` up to 120. Each entry has:

| Entry fields | Values |
| --- | --- |
| `id` | Unique entry identifier; 1–81 ASCII letters, digits, underscores or hyphens, beginning with a letter or digit. |
| `kind`, `status` | Kind: `sale`, `purchase`, `expense` or `extra`. Status: `draft`, `confirmed` or `cancelled`; a confirmed sale is displayed as issued. |
| `number`, `party`, `issuer`, `taxId` | Strings, at most 160 Unicode code points each. |
| `address`, `issuerAddress`, `note`, `cancelReason` | Strings, at most 1,000 Unicode code points each. |
| `date`, `due`, `currency` | Empty string or valid `YYYY-MM-DD` date (0001–9999); due date cannot precede the record date when both exist. Currency is empty or three uppercase ASCII letters. |
| `category` | `service`, `supplies`, `food`, `transport`, `health`, `equipment`, `insurance` or `other`. |
| `lines` | 1–100 objects with exactly `description` (string, up to 500 code points), `quantity` (integer 1–10,000), `unitMinor` (integer hundredths or `null`) and `bookingId` (empty or an identifier in the same format as entry IDs). Booking references do not create or update daily records. |
| `vatMinor` | Optional included tax, represented by integer hundredths or `null`; cannot exceed a known total. |
| `sourceId`, `region`, `raw` | Empty or a document ID; `null` or `{ "page": <integer 1–100>, "x": <fraction>, "y": <fraction>, "width": <fraction>, "height": <fraction> }`; recognized text up to 20,000 code points. Regions require a source, use 0–1 coordinates from the top left, and must have positive width/height within the page. The UI displays percentages and limits PDF intake to 20 pages. |
| `payments` | 0–100 objects with exactly `id` (unique within the entry, same identifier format), `date` (valid calendar date), `amountMinor` (positive integer hundredths) and `note` (string up to 500 code points). |

Text is retained literally; newline, carriage return and tab are allowed, while other ASCII controls, DEL and lone surrogate code points are rejected. Known unit prices and included tax range from zero through 100,000,000 hundredths; individual payments range from 1 through 100,000,000 hundredths. An entry total cannot exceed 1,000,000,000,000 hundredths. Missing line prices make the total unknown.

Confirmation requires a date, nonblank party, currency, known total and nonblank description on every line. Sales additionally require a nonblank invoice number, client address, issuer and issuer address. Non-draft sale numbers must be unique within the business after trimming surrounding whitespace, including cancelled sales; numbering is entered manually. Once a sale leaves draft status, its details remain fixed; an unpaid confirmed sale may only transition to cancelled with a reason. Existing payments must be retained unchanged, and payments require a confirmed record with a sufficient known total. A source/page/region combination cannot be reused by another non-cancelled entry.

Each `documents` item has exactly `{ "id": <sha256>, "name": <original filename>, "type": <MIME type>, "size": <byte count>, "sha256": <sha256> }`. Both hash fields must be identical 64-character lowercase hexadecimal SHA-256 values. Filenames are nonblank, at most 180 code points; supported types are `application/pdf`, `image/jpeg` and `image/png`. Retain existing metadata unchanged; browser validation also preserves each metadata object's field order. For each new document, include exactly one `{ "id": <sha256>, "data": <strict base64 without a data-URL prefix> }` in `uploads`. Re-uploading an existing ID is rejected. The server checks file signatures, byte counts, hashes and the 5 MiB/file, 10 files/20 MiB/write limits; the shared 32 MiB JSON body cap also includes snapshot text and base64 overhead.

A successful write returns the full account state with the next shared revision and closes legacy import eligibility, even for an empty finance snapshot. Failed validation returns 400; database failures return 500. Both roll back records, originals, revision and import eligibility. Shared session/origin/precondition errors follow [README errors](../README.md#errors). Download originals with the session cookie at `GET /api/finance-documents/<sha256>`; missing or other-business files return 404. Responses use no-store, nosniff, sandbox and attachment headers. Direct downloads use `document-<sha256>.<extension>`; the UI restores the recorded filename.

The authoritative validators are [finance-model.js](../finance-model.js) and [api/finance.py](../api/finance.py). Keep issued details, existing entries, original metadata and payment history when constructing a replacement snapshot.

## Local readers and checks

Bundled versions/licenses/checksums: [local document readers](../assets/vendor/finance/README.md). Only intake/export loads those libraries. Core app/API remains build-free with no runtime Python dependencies.

Relevant automated checks:

```sh
node --test tests/test_finance_model.js tests/test_finance_documents.js tests/test_finance_export.js
python3 -m unittest tests.test_finance_api tests.test_booking_cancellation -v
uv run --python 3.12 --with playwright==1.61.0 python -m unittest tests.test_finance_browser tests.test_billing_browser -v
```

These checks use synthetic invoices/receipts and disposable stores. They cover invoice/extra/payment save/reload, literal Excel cells and totals, two-receipt local OCR/classification, byte-identical original export, duplicate rejection, account isolation, revision/atomicity guards, all locales and routes at desktop/tablet/phone dimensions. Billing/cancellation checks cover historical family attribution after dog reassignment, server-priced carer bookings and cancellation's invoice guards.

The browser suite runs the bundled OCR on synthetic raster card slips and invoice-reference fixtures in both modes. It checks that a sparse **MASTERCARD** slip keeps its supplier/reference/tax gaps while retaining its date, amount, currency and recognized text; confirmation requires completing the supplier. Reference fixtures check that field labels leave references blank, that a supported invoice number is proposed, and that manually reviewed values survive save/reload. Export checks independently parse the XLSX XML for numeric amounts, literal references and source links, and compare archived originals byte for byte. `DOGCARE_EVIDENCE_DIR` retains selected phone/desktop review screenshots. These tests populate file inputs and inspect the camera hint; they do not exercise a physical camera or establish real-device capture compatibility. Full repository acceptance remains required before release.
